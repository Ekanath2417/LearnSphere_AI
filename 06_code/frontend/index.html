02 / Workflow

A repeatable study loop.

The interface keeps the student close to the work and away from clutter.

Capture

Add subjects, notes, syllabus, files, timetable slots, and previous questions.

Plan

Create focused tasks with estimated minutes and simple priority visibility.

Practice

Use short checks and revision prompts to find gaps early.

Reflect

Record diary entries and use patterns to make a better next plan.

03 / Trust

Private-by-design learning guidance.

Provider credentials belong only in server-side secret management or approved OAuth flows. LearnSphere AI must never ask a student for a third-party account password.

AI outputs are treated as study support. Predicted marks are indicative learning signals and are not certain, diagnostic, or official academic judgments.
